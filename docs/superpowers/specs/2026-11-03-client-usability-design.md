# Client usability refinement

User authorization: “go” after the research-informed usability proposal; proceed with sensible defaults without another design interview.

Goal: prospective clients with different technical confidence can understand the offer, find relevant examples and contact P2 without needing technical vocabulary. Existing clients retain the public contact channel; no account or support portal is introduced.

Approaches considered: (1) copy-only cleanup is low risk but leaves inquiry friction; (2) refine the existing six-page site and inquiry handoff, chosen because it addresses comprehension and completion; (3) rebuild or add a service wizard increases maintenance and adds decisions before contact. Choice 2 is the implementer's call under the user's go-ahead.

Preserve the existing logo, palette, typography, concept disclosures, service boundaries, no public pricing and optional care model. Keep current routes and deployment configuration. No domain, operator identity, delivery receiver or response-time promises are invented. Do not publish as part of this refinement.

Navigation: Services, Our work, How it works, About, Contact us. Reuse the same language on all six pages. Services lead with client needs: a business website, everyday-work tools, selling online and website care. Explain technical details in everyday terms on detail pages.

Inquiry: name, email, description and consent required; business/project name becomes optional with other scoping questions. Describe the problem before optional fields. Use a single-column form. Default action “Review email draft” prepares a visible read-only draft, recipient, open-email action, copy action and downloadable backup. Only the visitor sends; copy success never implies delivery. Copy failure selects the draft for manual copying. Editing a field invalidates prepared output. Direct-delivery mode retains its existing explicit sending and failure behavior.

Accessibility: persistent labels, associated guidance/errors, visible focus, native selects/details, readable mobile text and generous controls. No motion-dependent information. No external dependency or tracker.

Evidence: W3C clear words and understandable content, NN/G recognition and status feedback, Baymard form-effort research (checkout findings are a hypothesis for this inquiry form), GOV.UK moderated testing with likely users. These inform decisions; they do not prove this site's conversion or comprehension. Write a short target-user testing protocol; do not claim research participants were tested.

Acceptance: tests and build pass; desktop and 320/390px mobile walkthroughs cover navigation, service details, invalid/minimal inquiry, optional phone validation, preview/copy/recovery, invalidation and dialog keyboard use. Check representative sibling pages and privacy. Record observed limits and provide screenshots and local preview.

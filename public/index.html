Common questions

I’m not sure what I need. Can I still get in touch?

Yes. Tell us about your business and what you want to improve. You do not need to choose a service or prepare a technical brief before contacting us.

Do I have to pay a monthly subscription?

No. We agree on a project fee for the work. Monthly care is a separate, optional service. Hosting, domain renewals, and other third-party costs still need to be budgeted.

What will I own after the project?

Your written agreement explains which files, accounts, and rights you receive after payment, which parts use a separate license, and the instructions and access included when we hand over the site.

How long will my website take?

We’ll propose a schedule after reviewing your requirements. Content readiness, approvals, functionality, and integrations all affect the timeline. No launch date is promised before scoping.

Can you work on an existing website?

Request a review through the form below. We’ll first need to understand the technology, access, condition, and changes you want before agreeing on the work or support.

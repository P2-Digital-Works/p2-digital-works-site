A FEW THINGS, ANSWERED

Before we begin.

Do I have to pay a monthly subscription?

No. Development is scoped as a one-time project. Monthly care is a separate, optional service. Hosting, domain renewals, and other third-party costs still need to be budgeted.

What will I own after the project?

Your written agreement will specify the deliverables and rights transferred after payment, the components licensed to you, and the source files, access, and documentation included in handover.

How long will my website take?

We’ll propose a schedule after reviewing your requirements. Content readiness, approvals, functionality, and integrations all affect the timeline. No launch date is promised before scoping.

Can you work on an existing website?

Request a review through the form below. We’ll first need to understand the technology, access, condition, and changes you want before confirming scope or support.

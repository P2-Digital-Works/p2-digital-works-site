Your information,
your inquiry.

This page explains how the P2 Digital Works website handles the details you choose to share.

What you provide

The inquiry form asks for your name, email, business or project name, and a project description. You may also provide your interested service, timeframe, budget context, business type, or phone number. Please do not include passwords, financial account details, patient information, or other sensitive material.

When you continue to email

The form prepares a message addressed to P2 Digital Works in your own email app. You review and send that message yourself. Preparing the message does not send it. Your email service handles the message under its own terms and settings.

How we use an inquiry

Information you send is used to respond, understand your requirements, and discuss a possible project. Sending an inquiry does not commit you to a purchase or subscribe you to marketing.

Website storage and tracking

This website does not use analytics or advertising trackers and does not save form entries to browser storage. The form uses your entries in the current page. Your browser may retain entries through its normal autofill or page-restoration features. If you download a brief, that copy is saved on your device. Messages you send by email remain subject to the storage practices of the email services involved.

Questions about your information

To ask about an inquiry you have sent, or to request correction or deletion of information you shared with P2 Digital Works, write to paulrodolf.castor@g.msuiit.edu.ph. Do not send additional sensitive information just to identify your inquiry.

Support with clear boundaries

Care starts with understanding the website: its technology, hosting, current condition, access, and the changes you expect. We review that context before agreeing on ongoing responsibilities.

Define what is included

A care agreement can cover applicable updates, technical checks, backup arrangements, support, and a limited amount of content work. Response arrangements, edit allowances, exclusions, and third-party fees are agreed separately. New features are scoped as new projects.

Scope we can discuss

  • Technical checks and applicable updates
  • Backups and recovery planning where supported
  • A defined allowance for support and content changes

Deliverables, timing, project fees, and client responsibilities are defined in a written proposal.
